Maintenance Schedules by System Type

Every type of water treatment system has different maintenance needs. We track each one independently and coordinate service visits for efficiency.

Water Softeners

Semi-annual hardness testing, annual resin assessment, salt delivery as needed. Rebed every 5-10 years.

Learn more

Carbon Filtration

Quarterly chlorine/taste testing, annual media assessment. Rebed every 1-3 years depending on volume.

Learn more

Reverse Osmosis

Pre-filters every 3-6 months, membrane inspection annually, TDS monitoring quarterly. Membrane replacement every 2-4 years.

Learn more

DI Systems

Conductivity monitoring monthly to quarterly, resin replacement based on exhaustion. Portable exchange on scheduled intervals.

Learn more

UV Sterilization

Annual lamp replacement, quartz sleeve cleaning semi-annually, UV intensity monitoring.

Cost Savings of Preventive Maintenance

Maintenance contracts aren't an expense — they're an investment that pays for itself many times over.

Equipment Lifespan

Proper maintenance extends the life of commercial water treatment equipment by 40-60%. A system that lasts 15 years instead of 10 saves thousands in replacement costs.

Emergency Repair Avoidance

Emergency commercial water system repairs cost 2-3x more than scheduled maintenance. Proactive service prevents the failures that cause emergency calls.

Downstream Equipment Protection

When water treatment fails, it damages everything downstream — ice machines, boilers, dishwashers, steamers, laboratory equipment. Maintenance protects your entire water-using equipment investment.

Compliance & Documentation

Healthcare, food service, and manufacturing facilities need documented water quality records. Our maintenance reports provide audit-ready documentation of every service visit.

How We Set Up Your Maintenance Plan

From assessment to ongoing service — a simple, structured approach.

1

System Assessment

We evaluate every component of your water treatment system — filtration, softening, RO, DI, UV — and create a maintenance plan specific to your equipment and water conditions.

2

Custom Maintenance Schedule

Based on your system types, water quality, and usage volume, we build a maintenance calendar. Every service visit is planned in advance — no surprises, no forgotten filters.

3

Scheduled Service Visits

Our technicians arrive on schedule, perform all planned maintenance, test water quality before and after service, and document everything in a written report.

4

Ongoing Monitoring & Adjustment

We track trends in your water quality data over time. If conditions change — water source changes, usage increases, regulations update — we adjust your plan accordingly.
