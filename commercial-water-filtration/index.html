Industries We Serve

Every industry has unique water quality requirements. We engineer commercial water treatment solutions specific to your operation — not generic residential systems scaled up.

Restaurants & Food Service

Chlorine-free water improves food and beverage taste. Protect ice machines, steamers, and dishwashers from scale buildup that drives up repair costs and health code risks.

Medical & Healthcare Facilities

Autoclaves, sterilizers, and lab equipment require purified water to function properly. We provide RO, DI, and softening systems that meet healthcare compliance standards.

Manufacturing & Industrial

Process water quality directly affects product consistency. Our commercial filtration systems deliver the water purity your production line demands — from rinse water to boiler feed.

Car Washes & Detailing

Spot-free rinse water requires deionized or RO-treated water. We design and maintain DI and RO systems sized for high-volume car wash operations throughout South Florida.

Breweries & Beverage Production

Water chemistry is the foundation of great beer and beverages. Our commercial RO and carbon systems give you complete control over your water profile — chlorine-free, mineral-adjusted, consistent.

HOAs, Hotels & Multi-Family Properties

Protect common-area plumbing, reduce tenant complaints, and extend the life of water heaters and fixtures across your property with commercial-grade softening and filtration.

Commercial Water Filtration Systems

We install and service every type of commercial water treatment system. Each is engineered to your facility's specific water chemistry, flow demands, and operational requirements.

Commercial Carbon Filtration

Catalytic carbon systems remove chlorine, chloramines, VOCs, and taste/odor issues at commercial flow rates. Essential for restaurants, hotels, and any business where water taste matters.

Carbon Rebed Service

Commercial Water Softening

High-capacity ion exchange softeners eliminate hard water scale that damages commercial equipment. Sized for your facility's flow rate and hardness level — typically 14-22 GPG in South Florida.

Softener Rebed Service

Commercial Reverse Osmosis

Commercial RO systems produce purified water for laboratories, medical facilities, food production, and beverage operations. Multi-stage membrane filtration removes 98%+ of dissolved solids.

RO Maintenance Service

Deionization (DI) Systems

Mixed-bed and separate-bed DI systems produce ultra-pure water for car washes, labs, electronics manufacturing, and pharmaceutical applications. Portable exchange and fixed-bed options available.

DI Filter Service

UV Sterilization

Commercial UV systems provide chemical-free disinfection for facilities on well water or those requiring bacteria-free water for food prep, healthcare, or manufacturing processes.

Why Simple Water Service for Commercial

Simple Water Service is a family-owned water treatment company — three generations deep. We've been headquartered in West Palm Beach since 2008 and have completed over 15,000 installations across Palm Beach County and the Treasure Coast. We know South Florida water better than anyone because we've been living in it and working with it our entire lives.

For commercial clients, that local expertise matters. South Florida's water is uniquely challenging — hard water at 14-22 grains per gallon, aggressive chloramine treatment, high TDS, and (for businesses on well water) iron, sulfur, and bacteria. Generic water treatment companies from out of state don't understand these conditions the way we do.

We treat our commercial clients the same way we treat our residential ones: test first, recommend second, and stand behind every system with responsive service. No corporate call centers, no ticket queues — when you call Simple Water Service, you talk to someone who knows your system and your water.
