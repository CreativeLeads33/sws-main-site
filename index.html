South Florida Water Problems Are Real

Palm Beach County water is notoriously hard and often contaminated with iron, sulfur, and chlorine. Here's what homeowners deal with every day:

Hard Water Scale

White crusty buildup on faucets, showerheads, and inside pipes

Chemical Taste & Smell

Bleach-like taste and odor in tap water and ice

Iron & Sulfur

Orange staining, metallic taste, and rotten egg smell from well water

Dry Skin & Hair

Hard water and chlorine strip natural oils from skin and hair
