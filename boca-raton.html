Water Treatment in Boca Raton — What Every Homeowner Should Know

Boca Raton has some of the hardest municipal water in South Florida. The City of Boca Raton draws from the Surficial Aquifer and treats at its own water plant — but even after treatment, hardness levels consistently measure in the “very hard” category. Residents notice it immediately: white scale on everything, shortened appliance life, and a chemical taste that makes tap water undrinkable without a filter.

The Boca Raton water treatment challenge is compounded by the community's investment in premium homes. Custom shower enclosures cloud within weeks without a softener. Imported fixtures develop scale in hard-to-reach places. Gourmet kitchen ice makers produce cloudy ice. Luxury bathrooms develop rings. All of this is caused by untreated hard water — and all of it is preventable.

Our Boca Raton installations are designed for performance and aesthetics. We use compact, high-efficiency systems that fit in Florida garages without taking over the space. For homes with limited garage space — townhomes, smaller lots — we offer wall-mounted units and compact tank configurations.

We serve all Boca Raton communities — from the estates in Royal Palm Yacht & Country Club to the townhomes in Boca West, from Mission Bay to Woodfield Country Club. Every installation includes a free water test, written results, same-day quote, and next-day installation availability.
