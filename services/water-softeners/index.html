Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Palm Beach County homeowners.

That slick feeling isn't salt in the water — softeners don't add salt to your water. Soft water has a different polarity that causes your skin to release excess oil it normally uses to repel hard water. Most homeowners fully acclimate within a few weeks and the feeling goes away. If you split time between homes (snowbirds, frequent travelers) and never fully acclimate, we recommend a blending valve so you can dial in exactly how soft the water feels.

A blending valve lets you blend a percentage of soft water with untreated water instead of going straight to 100% soft. It's about $100 added to your system and gives you full control — you can start at a lower blend and increase it over time as your household adjusts, rather than committing to full softness on day one.
