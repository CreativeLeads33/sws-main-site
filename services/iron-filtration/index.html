Common iron levels in PBC well water frequently exceed the EPA secondary standard of 0.3 ppm. Our AirGuard systems handle iron levels up to 15 ppm without chemicals.

Our Process

Simple, fast, and done at your home.

1

Iron & Manganese Test

We test your water for iron type (ferrous vs ferric), manganese, pH, and hydrogen sulfide to determine the right treatment approach.

2

AirGuard System Installation

Our AirGuard systems use air injection oxidation — no chemicals, no potassium permanganate, no chlorine injection.

3

Filtration & Backwash

Oxidized iron is trapped in the filter media and automatically flushed during the backwash cycle.

4

Post-Install Water Test

After installation, we re-test to confirm iron levels are below detection limits.
